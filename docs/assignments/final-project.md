# Final Project: Mapping Term Project

The final project is a mapping term project that brings together the skills from the labs: finding
data, building a workflow, running an analysis, and communicating the result with maps. It is
introduced in Week 12 alongside the site-selection lectures, and the last two weeks of the
semester are in-class work sessions with the instructors' help. It is due on the **Saturday of
Week 14** at 11:59 pm; the exact date is on Learning Suite.

## Deliverable

Submit your report as a **single PDF** on Learning Suite. Make sure to include:

- intermediate maps and data that show the data processing steps you went through, and
- a final map showing your results.

## Getting Started

- Review the workflow diagrams and the site-selection-as-elimination approach from
  [Day 22](../lectures/day-22.md) and [Day 23](../lectures/day-23.md).
- [Lab 11: Walmart Site Selection](lab-11/README.md) is the closest model for the kind of workflow
  the project expects.
- AI can help you plan a workflow and polish your writing; the QGIS work and the results must be
  your own. See the [AI Use Policy](../policies/ai-policy.md).
